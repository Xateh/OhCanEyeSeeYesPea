## Offline evidence scoring

assess accepts deterministic recorded receipts only. It preserves every planned case/arm/repetition slot, including missing, malformed JSON, explicit UNKNOWN, abstention, timeout, provider/tool error and incorrect evidence. It calls the original frozen output_verdict on the intended canonical instance, with raw/normalized agreement checks and the original 65,536-byte raw bound. A valid schedule earns success only with FEASIBLE status agreeing with gold and no operational failure. A timeout containing a valid schedule is still unsuccessful. No certificate vocabulary, solver status or repaired translation is promoted into success.

Receipt routing and shape reject duplicate/unplanned slots. Termination includes final, abstained, timeout, provider_error and tool_error; provider/tool error names require their matching flags. Abstention requires null raw/normalized output, empty normalization errors and no provider/tool error. Flags overlap and are not exclusive outcome bins. Summaries report raw planned/success/missing/unknown/error/timeout counts per arm and family/base; zero-success and missing attempts stay in planned denominators. No legacy or proposed answer-completion field, paired statistical inference, optimizer objective, live latency/cost or retry selection is introduced.
